🦷 Prime Dental

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Prime Dental is practice-management software for dental clinics, made by Dr. Saad Nazir ("we"). This page explains what happens to the information a clinic enters into the app, and what the optional Google Calendar and Google Contacts connections do.

The short version: Prime Dental runs on the clinic's own computer. Patient records are stored on that computer and are never sent to us. Patient details leave the computer only if the clinic chooses to connect its own Google Calendar or Google Contacts, or uses voice dictation (turned into text by Windows).

1. Where clinic data is stored

Everything a clinic enters — patients, visits, treatment notes, prescriptions, payments, appointments, stock and registers — is saved on the clinic's own Windows computer. We do not run a server that holds clinic data, and we cannot see, access or recover it.

Backups are written to a folder the clinic chooses. If the clinic picks a folder that is synced by a service such as OneDrive or Google Drive, that service's own terms apply to the backup files.

2. What the app sends over the internet

Prime Dental works fully offline. When the computer is online, it may make these connections:

Update checkAsks our release page on GitHub whether a newer version exists. No clinic or patient data is sent.
WhatsAppButtons such as "Remind" open WhatsApp with a message already typed. Nothing is sent until the clinic presses Send in WhatsApp.
Voice dictationWhen the clinic uses a microphone (🎤) button, the spoken words are turned into text by Microsoft's speech service built into Windows. They are not sent to or stored by us. Typing works fully offline.
Google CalendarOnly if the clinic turns it on — see section 3.
Google ContactsOnly if the clinic turns it on — see section 3.

Prime Dental has no analytics, advertising or usage tracking.

Licence activation is done by exchanging a short code with us (for example on WhatsApp). That code identifies the computer; it contains no patient data.

3. Google Calendar and Google Contacts (optional)

A clinic can connect its own Google account in Settings → Google Calendar so that appointments appear on the dentist's phone. It is off unless the clinic connects it, and it can be disconnected at any time from the same place.

What we ask Google for. When the clinic connects, Prime Dental requests two permissions: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — to create, change and delete events in the connected account's calendar; and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts — to save patients as contacts in the same account, used only if the clinic also ticks Also save patients to Google Contacts.

What we do with it. When an appointment is added or changed in Prime Dental, the app creates or updates a matching event in the account's primary calendar. When an appointment is cancelled, marked no-show or deleted, the app deletes that event. Each event contains:

  • patient name and the planned procedure and tooth number, or the appointment type when no procedure is linked (the event title)
  • date, time and the clinic name (the location)
  • the patient's phone number, the planned procedure and tooth, any appointment or follow-up notes, MR number and appointment status (the description). Fees are never sent.
  • a hidden reference number so the app can find the same event again

The app also reads the calendar's name once, to show the clinic which account is connected.

Google Contacts (separate switch, off by default). When turned on, the app saves each patient who has a phone number as a contact, so calls and WhatsApp messages on the clinic's phone show who is calling. Each contact contains only:

  • the patient's name and MR number (for example "Ali Khan (MR-P009)")
  • the patient's phone number
  • a hidden reference number so the app can find the same contact again

No medical, treatment or payment information is ever placed in a contact. When a patient's name or number changes in the app, the contact is updated. Deleting a patient in the app does not delete the contact.

Numbers the clinic has already saved. So that it never adds a second contact for a number the clinic has saved itself, the app reads the phone numbers only of the contacts in the connected account (not their names or any other details) and skips those numbers. Contacts the clinic made itself are never changed or deleted. The app deletes a contact only when the clinic presses Remove duplicate contacts and confirms, and then only contacts the app created for a number the clinic had already saved.

What we do not do. Prime Dental does not read, list, download, change or delete any other events in the calendar — only the ones it created — and does not change or delete any contacts it did not create (of those, it reads only the phone numbers, as described above). It does not access Gmail, Drive or any other Google service.

Where the data goes. Data travels directly between the clinic's computer and Google. It does not pass through any server of ours, and we never receive or store it. The sign-in token is kept only on that computer and is not included in backups or exports. Disconnecting in Settings revokes the token and deletes it from the computer. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events and contacts already created stay in your account until you delete them.

Limited Use. Prime Dental's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show the clinic's own appointments in its own calendar and its own patients in its own contacts. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone else, and not read by any person except the clinic itself.

Appointment details placed in Google Calendar and contacts placed in Google Contacts are held by Google under Google's own privacy policy. Clinics should only connect a Google account that belongs to the clinic.

4. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. The demo video is embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. The site uses no cookies or analytics of its own.

5. Patient data, clinic data and removal requests

The clinic is responsible for the patient data it records in Prime Dental and for deciding whether to connect Google Calendar or Google Contacts. We never receive patient records. A patient who wants to see, correct or remove their record should ask their clinic: the clinic can delete the patient in Prime Dental and remove any copies from its own backup files and its own Google account.

To issue and renew licences we keep only the clinic's name, a coded computer ID, the licence number and its dates. A clinic can ask us on WhatsApp to delete this when it no longer uses Prime Dental.
